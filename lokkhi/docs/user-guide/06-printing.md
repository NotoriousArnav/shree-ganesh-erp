# Printing

Open an issued invoice and choose **Print**. The browser print dialog opens with
the invoice laid out as a document: navigation and hints are hidden, and only the
invoice itself is printed.

Choose **Save as PDF** in the print dialog to keep a digital copy.

## What is printed

- invoice kind, number and date
- due date, when set
- customer or supplier name, phone and GSTIN
- every line with quantity, unit, price, discount, tax and total
- notes
- subtotal, discount, tax and total

## What is not printed

- the **Not in Mushak** and **Archived in Mushak** hints
- the **Add to Mushak** link
- stock sync notes and retry links
- navigation and action buttons

Hints are for you, not for the customer. The invoice document stays clean.

## Long invoices

The printed table is sized to the page, so the Total column is never cut off.
When an invoice runs past one page, the column headings repeat at the top of each
new page and rows are not split across pages. The totals block always appears at
the end, aligned to the right.

## Drafts

A draft prints without a number. Issue it first if the customer needs a numbered
bill.

## A printed copy never changes

Because each line stores its own snapshot, a PDF made today still shows the name,
price and tax from the day it was issued. Editing or deleting the Mushak product
later does not alter the printed invoice.
